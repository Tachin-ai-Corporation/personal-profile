Design direction A of 3 · Personal Profile

Chart

Reads like a well-kept medical record: a ruled label/value grid, hairline dividers and short small-caps headings, with status written as text tags rather than color. It suits patients and providers who scan for one fact and leave, and it borrows the authority of the paper chart. Edit mode opens up into roomier, form-sized rows, so the change from reading to editing is obvious.
