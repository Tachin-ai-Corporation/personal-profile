Design direction B of 3 · Personal Profile

Console

Treats the profile as a small command center: a dark, always-visible section rail reports the health of every section (status dot, count, one-line summary) before you scroll to it. Heavier type, compact cards and stat tiles put the data first, which suits providers who manage records daily and patients who want to see at a glance what needs attention. It is the most "1health platform" of the three, borrowing the dark command-center tokens for navigation only.
