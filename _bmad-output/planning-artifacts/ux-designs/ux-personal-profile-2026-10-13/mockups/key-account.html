What the frames show

  • A: picking the work address shows the login notice under the Select; it stays until Save or Cancel. The phone picker is aria-disabled because Daniel has one verified phone, and the helper links to the Contacts tab.
  • A: blank language and time zone read "Select a value" and must be picked before Save.
  • B and F: each tile is a button "Link {App}". Tile hover uses the row-hover fill (Microsoft in A/B).
  • D: a wrong code clears the field and keeps focus in it; the QR is not re-requested while the dialog is open.
  • E: after linking, the status reads "Enabled", Disable opens "Turn off authenticator app?", and the tiles are inactive with the reason as caption and Tooltip.
  • F: the tab strip scrolls sideways; tiles sit in a 3-column grid.
  • All frames: the app bar shows the page nav "Personal Profile | Account" with Account current; on F (390 px) it wraps under the wordmark as a segmented control.
  • A: the Birth date help is a "?" button beside the label (edit view only); its Tooltip is drawn open for review.
  • Not drawn: below 768 px the Link authenticator dialog opens full-screen (header with close, QR and code field scroll, Cancel + Submit pinned to the bottom). It is the only full-screen dialog; the Disable confirm stays centered with 16 px gutters.
